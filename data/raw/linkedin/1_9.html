      In 1988, we started our journey with a small store in Kuzguncuk, Istanbul. Today, we continue as a global fashion brand with over 7.900 employees, more than 450 stores, and online sales channels, operating in 72 countries.


      We are driven by our vision “With our strong brand fueled by our fast and agile business model, we will reach 1 billion customers worldwide with our products by 2030.”


      Guided by our mission “Making fashion trends accessible for the world”, we deliver trends to our customers just in time - in line with our motto, “Trend in Time.”


      At Koton, we build success on our values, because “Achieving success with our values is woven into our fabric!'"

      Ready to shape the future of talent and culture at one of Turkey’s leading fashion brands?


      At Koton, we don’t just set fashion trends we redefine how people grow, engage and thrive at work. We are looking for a Logistics Specialist who will play a pivotal role in building inspiring development journeys and positioning Koton as a top employer in the fashion industry.


      Qualifications

      • Bachelor’s degree,
      • Minimum 2 years’ experience in a similar position,
      • Planning, organization, and coordination abilities, with a proactive approach to decision-making,
      • Strong communication and interpersonal skills with a customer-oriented mindset,
      • Ability to teamwork, detail and result oriented, analytical, strong communication and human relations skills,
      • Good command of MS Office programs,
      • Proficiency in English.


      Responsibilities

      • Coordinates with the HQ team to confirm all shipping documents,
      • Communicate with the clearance agent and follows up on all clearance processes,
      • Prepares and maintains all logistics tracking reports such as customs clearance progress, local delivery status, warehouse and inventory levels, and logistics cost reports,
      • Verifies all logistics-related charges and payments in accordance with official and internal finance procedures,
      • Provides logistics solutions and organizes related operational activities to support internal sales and after-sales teams in customs clearance, warehousing, transportation, and logistics analysis,
      • Manages and maintains strong working relationships with internal stakeholders, local customs authorities, and relevant government departments,
      • Handles irregular or unexpected cases and continuously works to optimize existing logistics operations.
