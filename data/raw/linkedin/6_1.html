      About The Role

      To support our international growth and reinforce our position as a market leader in France, we are seeking a Front-End Developer (M/F) for a permanent position.

      As part of a team of 10 people, and reporting to the Operations Director, your mission will be responsible for developing and improving our clients’ digital advertising materials (desktop, mobile, video, native ads, rich-media, etc.).

      You will be at the heart of ADventori’s operations, collaborating closely with designers, campaign managers, and developers to deliver high-quality, technically sound advertising assets.

      Responsibilities and Duties

      At the Core of Digital Campaign Deployment, your key responsibilities will include:

      • Develop and optimize digital ad formats following brand guidelines and/or mockups.
      • Ensure technical compliance of received creatives (IAB standards, DCO integration, etc.).
      • Provide technical feedback and recommendations to partner agencies.
      • Collaborate with internal teams (campaign managers, tech, marketing) on ad production.
      • Contribute to continuous improvement of tools, templates, and workflows.
      • Document processes and technical specifications (in English and French).

      Qualifications

      Professional Competencies

      • 3 to 5 years of professional experience in front-end development.
      • Excellent skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
      • Strong expertise in web animation, responsive design.
      • Highly proficient with Figma and Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator) .
      • Comfortable using Git for version control.
      • Fluent in French and English (both written and spoken).
      • Strong attention to detail, organised, and able to manage multiple projects.
      • Team player with good communication skills.
      • Experience in digital advertising or UI/UX design is a plus.

      Job Details

      • Contract type: Permanent position.
      • Location: Remote (working on Paris time zone).
      • Start date: As soon as possible.

      Recruitment Process

      • First interview with the Operations Director.
      • Technical test to assess coding and problem-solving skills.
      • Second interview with the team.
      • Optional final interview depending on previous results.

      Cultural Competencies

      • Must be able to work primarily between the hours of 9:30 AM - 6:30 PM GMT+1
      • Must have a player/coach mentality, willing to both execute and lead
      • Ability to juggle multiple projects concurrently and set and meet deadlines
      • Skilled at receiving and providing direct feedback
      • Collaborative mindset and great teamwork skills
      • Entrepreneurial & adaptable; great learning skills
      • Curious, research-minded, data-informed
      • Able to search for possible solutions and apply them
      • Creative confidence
      • Proactive communicator
      • A sense of humor and a love of online culture
      • Thrives in a start-up culture
