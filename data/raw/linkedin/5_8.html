      RED TIC is recruiting for one of its partners a profile for EXPERT PLSQL.

      Responsibilities

      • Develop, test, and maintain PL/SQL packages, procedures, functions, and triggers for database applications.
      • Optimize and troubleshoot existing PL/SQL code to improve performance.
      • Collaborate with application developers, database administrators, and other stakeholders to integrate database solutions.
      • Design and implement data models and database structures.
      • Ensure data integrity and security across the database.
      • Participate in code reviews and contribute to best practices.
      • Document technical specifications and processes.
      • Provide support for database-related issues.

      Qualifications

      • Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
      • Proven experience as a PL/SQL Developer, preferably with 8 years of experience.
      • Strong knowledge of Oracle database technologies and SQL.
      • Experience in performance optimization and PL/SQL code tuning.
      • Familiarity with data modeling principles and database design.
      • Good understanding of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
      • Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
      • Ability to work in a team and communicate effectively.
